ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

Knowledge brings
us together.

Quiz Competition Organization (QCO) is an independent, student-led knowledge community established in 2025 in Bangladesh.

OUR STORY

A community built around curiosity.

QCO creates opportunities for learners to explore ideas, test their knowledge and build confidence through fair and friendly quiz competitions. Our goal is to make learning engaging and accessible.

We believe knowledge grows when people learn together, ask thoughtful questions and respect one another.
